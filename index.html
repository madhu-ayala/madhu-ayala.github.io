Madhushree Ayalasomayajula

PhD Candidate in Finance

University of Lausanne and Swiss Finance Institute

I am a PhD candidate in Finance at the University of Lausanne and the Swiss Finance Institute. My research interests are Asset Pricing, Sustainable Finance, Investments, and Asset Management.

In my job market paper, I show that the path of climate policy matters for whether carbon-intensive firms earn higher or lower expected returns. My other work studies how sustainable investors can drive corporate transition through engagement, and how the entry of active ETFs reallocates capital across mutual funds.

Previously, I worked for four years in consulting and financial services.
